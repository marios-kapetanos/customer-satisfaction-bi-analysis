# Customer Satisfaction & Loyalty Analysis

### Business Intelligence | Excel • Power Query • Power BI

## Project Overview

This project explores customer purchasing behavior, satisfaction levels, and loyalty program participation through data analysis and interactive business intelligence dashboards.

The objective is to identify sales patterns across product categories, geographic locations, and purchase methods, while examining customer satisfaction and loyalty.

Developed as part of a Business Intelligence & Data Visualization coursework project.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Timeline
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformations, calculated columns, and aggregations
- **Power BI Desktop:** Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, geographic maps, and data visualization

## Dataset

The analysis is based on a dataset containing:

- 5,000 transactions
- 996 unique customers
- Five product categories
- Five Greek cities
- Customer satisfaction scores ranging from 1 to 5
- Online and in-store purchase records
- Loyalty program participation information

## Data Preparation & Analysis

The workflow included:

1. Reviewing data quality, missing values, and data types.
2. Preparing date-related fields for time-based analysis.
3. Creating customer satisfaction categories.
4. Aggregating sales and satisfaction across business dimensions.
5. Developing Excel Pivot Tables and interactive dashboards.
6. Building Power BI reports with geographic sales and satisfaction visualizations.

## Key Findings

- **Total Sales:** €550,340.15
- **Total Transactions:** 5,000
- **Average Customer Satisfaction:** Approximately 3.01/5
- Beauty Products generated the highest total sales among the product categories.
- Athens recorded the highest total sales among the five cities.
- Online and in-store sales were distributed almost equally.
- Loyalty program members contributed slightly higher total sales, while average satisfaction was similar between members and non-members.
- Customer satisfaction varied relatively little across product categories.

## Key Learning Outcomes

- Applying Power Query transformations to prepare structured datasets.
- Developing interactive Excel and Power BI dashboards.
- Using geographic visualizations to communicate location-based performance.
- Designing KPI-driven business intelligence reports.
- Translating transaction-level data into actionable business insights.
